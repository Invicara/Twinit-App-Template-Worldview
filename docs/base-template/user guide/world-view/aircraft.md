# Aircraft

The **AIRCRAFT** panel sits under SITES. The checkbox is amber. It starts off, and it stays disabled while your view covers more than about 25 square degrees.

1. Zoom in until the panel no longer says `ZOOM IN UNTIL THE VIEW IS 25 SQ° OR LESS TO ENABLE AIRCRAFT.`
2. Turn the checkbox on.
3. Expand the panel if you want the list.

`ACQUIRING AIRBORNE TRACKS` means the first request is in flight. The list then shows a callsign (or a stand-in when the track has none), flight level, and speed in knots. The count on the header is the number of tracks in this view, up to a cap of 250.

Select a row, or select the aircraft marker on the globe, to highlight that track. A short trail follows it. The layer refreshes about every 30 seconds while it is on. Tracks that leave the view drop off the list.

Turn the checkbox off when you are done. The client stops asking for tracks.

`NO AIRBORNE TRACKS IN VIEW` means the request succeeded and nothing airborne was in the box. Pan to a busier region.

`UNABLE TO ACQUIRE AIRCRAFT` means the request failed. Check that you are still signed in and that a project is selected, then toggle the layer again.

This layer is live air traffic in the camera box.
